----------------------------------------------------
WHY WE ARE RECORDING
----------------------------------------------------
We are a student/research team building a Voice
Advisory System that helps farmers get agricultural
information in Hindi using their voice.

To make the system understand natural speech, we need
to record Hindi-speaking volunteers reading sentences
and asking farming questions. Your voice recording
will be used to:

  1. Train the speech recognition model to better
     understand Hindi as spoken in Odisha.
  2. Test whether the system gives correct and safe
     farming advice.
  3. Improve the system over time.

This is a research / educational project. The system
is NOT yet available commercially.

----------------------------------------------------
WHAT WILL BE RECORDED
----------------------------------------------------
- Your voice (audio only — no video)
- Duration per session: approximately 15-30 minutes
- Contents: scripted agricultural sentences + your
  own freely asked farming questions

No personally identifying information (face, name,
ID) will be stored along with the audio files.
Your recording will be stored using only a speaker
code (e.g., S01) and cannot be traced back to you
by anyone outside the project team.

----------------------------------------------------
WHERE YOUR AUDIO IS STORED
----------------------------------------------------
- On the project team's computer (password protected)
- Backed up to a private, password-protected cloud
  folder (Google Drive / OneDrive)
- Access is limited strictly to the project team
- Audio files are NOT posted on the internet or
  shared with any third party

Retention period: Audio will be deleted or fully
anonymised within 12 months of the project end,
or earlier if you request deletion.
